AI
3 · Understand intentAI recognizes visit notes, tasks or opportunity changes, or asks a clarifying question.
AI
4 · Extract & askAI pulls out facts and asks sales-expert questions for gaps.
AI
5 · Match & validateCustomer, open visit and opportunity IDs matched against live CRM data. No guessing.
NO AI · RULES & DATA
6 · You confirmVIVA reads back every planned change; you say “yes”. Nothing is written without your OK.
HUMAN

AI
Record lookup, visit detection, linking and the CRM writes themselves run on classic business logic, with no AI involved. VIVA never changes the CRM without your confirmation first.

🤝 Why not just Joule?
Joule is SAP’s general assistant: answers, summaries, drafted emails. VIVA is a focused visit-debrief copilot: hands-free voice dialogue, sales-expert questions, visits, tasks and opportunities created or updated after your OK, transcript attached. It complements Joule; to be aligned with SAP’s roadmap.
🔌 Not on SAP? No problem.
Modular integration layer for any CRM via standard APIs. Next step: pre-visit briefings and proactive “you promised X last time” reminders, all by voice.
